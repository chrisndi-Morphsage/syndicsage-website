Features

A complete platform for professional syndic management.

One platform covering the full administrative lifecycle of a Belgian VME — from payment follow-up to assembly documentation.

Structured Payment Reminders

Four escalation levels — from initial notice to formal referral — drafted consistently in EN, FR, or NL. Prepared as drafts for your review and approval before sending.

General Assembly Management

Structured agendas following the Belgian legal order. Vote recording and procuration management — including the two-mandate limit — with minutes prepared for your review.

Expense Tracking and Reconciliation

Structured expense ledger with budget comparison. Invoice data is pre-filled from uploaded documents for your confirmation. Bank CSV import from ING, BNP, KBC, and Belfius.

Maintenance and Compliance Reminders

Recurring obligations tracked with scheduled alerts ahead of due dates. Boiler inspections, fire certifications, and technical deadlines — monitored with a clear history of actions taken.

Centralised Document Storage

Per-building document vault, organised by category and securely accessible. Insurance policies, contracts, and assembly minutes — always available, always traceable.

Co-Owner Portal

A private access point for each co-owner. Their account balance, building documents, and assembly minutes — accessible via a secure link, without requiring account creation.

Belgian VME Law Reference

Submit questions about Belgian co-ownership law and receive structured responses with references to the Wet van 18 juni 2018 — in your preferred language.

Insurance Claim Management

Structured claim intake covering incident details, supporting attachments, policy references, and contact tracking — maintained in a single, auditable record.

Compliance and Deadline Tracking

Assembly deadlines, mandate expiry, and technical certifications tracked centrally and flagged ahead of time — so obligations are met consistently and on record.

What is an AG Meeting session?
Each session provides a secure video meeting room for your General Assembly — with live voting per agenda item, proxy enforcement, and real-time attendance tracking. When the meeting ends, SyndicSage prepares a draft procès-verbal from the transcript and vote results for your review. You then choose to send it to all co-owners or absentees only, directly from the platform.
Saves per session:
✓ ~2h writing the PV
✓ Manual vote tallying
✓ Attendance tracking
✓ Sending to absentees
